Mae's Story

Seated at my desk, I contemplated the assignement that our instructor had given. How, I wondered, could I describe a day that was so vividly painful yet still murky in details? Resolved not to make it my life's work, I began writing...


It was about seven o'clock on a Friday morning, a few weeks before Christmas. I'd spent the better part of the night behind the wheel of my eighteen wheeler fighting both a blustering snowstorm and a tremendous urge to fall asleep. A light dusting of snow on the ground here in Maybrook made the nightmare of my trip from Cleveland seem unreal.


Still tired after turning in the truck and paper work, I decided to stop for a cup of coffee before heading home. Instead of going to the diner, though, I headed straight for the place I always did at the end of a run, the Roadside Inn the local gin mill.

My friend, Nancy, was tending bar and it looked like I was her first customer. I ordered my coffee, she poured and we caught up on the latest gossip. It was pretty quiet at that time of the morning, so I put some change in the juke box, chose my favorite tunes and got comfortable. After about my third cup of coffee, I saw White Rabbit coming in the back door. No, White Rabbit's not his real name. His real name is Roger, but his hair is all white, which is how he got his CB handle. We always called each other by our CB handles. My handle was Little Star, but everybody called me Star. I liked it that way.
